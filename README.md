# European Football Career Simulator

A football career simulation game where the player creates one footballer and experiences their career from academy level through professional football.

## Core Concept
- **Single Player Focus**: You control only your own footballer.
- **Living World**: The rest of the football world is simulated by AI.
- **Match Involvement**: Play only the key moments and highlights.
- **Dynamic Career**: Decisions, performance, and injuries shape your unique story.

## V1 Scope
- Focus on major European leagues (England, Spain, Germany, Italy, France).
- Academy to First Team progression.
- Basic transfer and loan systems.
- Performance-based manager trust.

## Development Stack
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Architecture**: Modular Simulation Engine
- **Character System**: Procedural Seed-based Generation
